Privacy

Loopwork Games · plain-language privacy notice

Last updated: DRAFT 2026-11-22 · Draft, not yet reviewed. This page is a plain description of what our games do; it is not legal advice.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no cookies set by us.
  • Your scores, streaks, settings and Daily progress are saved only in your own browser (localStorage). We never receive them.
  • We don't sell or share personal data, because we don't collect any.

What is stored, and where

Each game (Triangulate, Outnumber, Twotone, Orbit Hop, Gridword, Hexclaim, KABLOCK!) and this hub save small bits of state in your browser's localStorage: things like best scores, Daily results, streaks, sound/theme settings, and which games you have opened on the hub (arcade.played). This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings (or use a private window). Clearing it resets your stats.

Computer opponents are bots that run in your browser. There are no other people in your match and nothing about your play is uploaded.

Analytics

The pages we host ourselves (on GitHub Pages) use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly, cookieless page-view counter, to count anonymous visits per page, plus rough, non-identifying information such as referrer, country, browser and screen size. It sets no cookies, does not build a profile of you, and does not use your IP address to track you across sites. It is not included in the builds we publish on game portals such as itch.io or CrazyGames. The GoatCounter dashboard is operated by us at loopwork.goatcounter.com; see goatcounter.com/privacy for how the service works.

Hosting

These pages are static files served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address in its server logs to deliver and secure the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The games load no fonts, scripts or images from other sites.

Our games on other sites

Some of our games are also published on platforms such as itch.io and CrazyGames. When you play there, that platform runs the page and may use its own cookies, accounts, analytics or ads under its own privacy policy. Please read their policies; this page covers only the pages we host ourselves.

Children

The games are general-audience and need no personal information to play. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If what we collect changes (for example when analytics is switched on), we will update this page and its date first.
